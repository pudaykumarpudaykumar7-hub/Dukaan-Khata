Privacy Policy

UVMS DUKAAN KHATA

Last updated: 26 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how UVMS DUKAAN KHATA (“we”, “our” or “the app”) collects, uses, stores and shares information when shop owners and their customers use the app and related services.

1. Information we may collect

  • Account information: information associated with Google Sign-In, such as your email address and basic account identifier.
  • Shop and business information: shop name, owner name, contact details, UPI ID and settings you choose to provide.
  • Customer and ledger information: customer names, phone numbers, account balances, bills, items, transactions, payments, returns and expense records entered by a shop owner.
  • Customer access information: phone number and PIN or other login details used for customer access.
  • Technical information: device and app information, diagnostic data and notification tokens needed to operate, secure and troubleshoot the service.

Please do not enter information that is not needed for your shop records. Shop owners are responsible for having an appropriate lawful basis and giving required notices before entering their customers’ personal information.

2. How we use information

We use information to provide account sign-in, maintain shop and customer ledgers, display bills and payment history, enable customer access, deliver notifications, provide subscription and payment features, protect the service, troubleshoot errors and respond to support or data requests.

3. Storage and service providers

Information may be processed or stored on the device and through service providers used to operate the app, including Google authentication services, Firebase services, Supabase services and Razorpay payment services where those features are enabled. These providers process information under their own terms and privacy policies. We do not publish your private shop ledger records for public access.

4. Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Information may be shared with service providers only as needed to operate the app, with the shop owner and the relevant customer to provide ledger features, when required by law, or to protect users and the service. Payment details may be handled directly by the payment provider; we do not intend to store full card or banking credentials.

5. Notifications

If you allow notifications, the app may use device notification tokens to send relevant account, bill or customer updates. You can manage notification permissions in your device settings.

6. Security and retention

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures intended to protect information. No online service or storage method can be guaranteed completely secure. We retain information for as long as needed to provide the service, meet legal obligations, resolve disputes and enforce our agreements. Shop owners should maintain their own appropriate backups of important business records.

7. Your choices and data deletion

You may stop using the app and may request access, correction or deletion of personal information by contacting us at the email below. Please include the account email or phone number associated with the request and identify the relevant shop, where applicable. We may need to verify your identity before acting. Some records may need to be retained where required by law or for legitimate accounting, security or dispute-resolution purposes. A customer may also contact the shop owner who manages that customer's ledger.

8. Children

The app is intended for business use by shop owners and their customers, not for children to independently create accounts. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us so we can review the request.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when our app or legal requirements change. The revised policy will be published on this page with an updated date.
