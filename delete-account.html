Account and Data Deletion

App: UVMS DUKAAN KHATA

This page explains how shop owners and customers can request deletion of their account and associated personal data.

How to request deletion

  1. Send an email to pudaykumarpudaykumar7@gmail.com with the subject UVMS DUKAAN KHATA - Account/Data Deletion Request.
  2. Include the account's registered email address (for an owner) or registered mobile number and shop name (for a customer). Do not include your password or PIN.
  3. Tell us whether you are requesting deletion of an owner account, a customer account, or specific records.
  4. We may contact you to verify that you own or are authorised to request deletion of the account or data.

What data may be deleted

Depending on the verified request and the account relationship, deletion may include owner profile and shop details, customer profile and contact details, bills, items, balances, payment and transaction records, expense and return records, and associated account information stored for the app.

Users can also delete supported individual records in the app using its delete controls (including long-press where available). Deleting a customer or record may also delete its associated bills, payments, or transaction history as described in the app.

Processing and retention

We aim to process verified deletion requests within 30 days. Some information may be retained for up to 90 days in backup systems before being overwritten or deleted. Information may be retained longer where required by law, for accounting, fraud prevention, security, or to resolve disputes. Any retained information will be restricted to that purpose and deleted when no longer required.

Contact

For deletion requests or questions, contact pudaykumarpudaykumar7@gmail.com.
